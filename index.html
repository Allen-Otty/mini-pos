KRA eTIMS (Tax Fiscalization)

Checking status...

Every sale automatically builds a KRA-compliant fiscalization payload and stores it, ready to submit once your OSCU/VSCU device key is connected. Actual submission to KRA is not yet live — this is the structural groundwork so it's a quick switch-on once you have device credentials.

Platform Admin — Switch Business

While switched, every tab (Sell, Catalog, Dashboard, Settings, M-Pesa, eTIMS...) operates on that business's real data for testing. Nothing you do here is visible to other businesses' users.
